Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 14, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how LumaRing Hop handles information when you use the game. LumaRing Hop is a single-player arcade game. You do not need to create an account to play.

2. Information We Store on Your Device

The game may store gameplay data locally on your device, including level progress, earned stars, music settings, sound effect settings, and other basic preferences. This data is used to keep your game progress and settings available between sessions.

3. Information Collected Automatically

LumaRing Hop may use third-party advertising and security services. These services may process device and usage information, such as device model, operating system version, app version, approximate region, advertising identifiers when permitted, diagnostic data, ad interaction data, and anti-fraud signals.

The game may request advertising configuration from https://ad-call.de123.net/app/ad/units using the app bundle identifier. This request is used to determine which advertising units are available for the current version of the app.

4. Advertising and App Tracking Transparency

LumaRing Hop may show optional rewarded ads after gameplay. The game may integrate advertising services including Chartboost and Yandex Ads. These providers may use information to load ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and deliver relevant advertising.

On iOS, access to the Identifier for Advertisers is requested through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. If you decline tracking permission, the game will not access the Identifier for Advertisers for tracking purposes. You can change your choice later in the iOS Settings app.

5. Security Services

The game may use a security SDK to help protect requests, validate app integrity, and reduce fraud or abuse. Security services may process technical signals needed to provide these protections.

6. How We Use Information

  • To save your game progress and preferences.
  • To provide gameplay features, rewarded ads, and audio settings.
  • To request advertising configuration for supported ad providers.
  • To detect errors, protect the app, and prevent fraud or misuse.
  • To comply with platform, legal, and App Store requirements.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers that help operate the app, such as advertising networks, analytics or diagnostic services if enabled, and security providers. These providers process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

8. Your Choices

  • You can enable or disable music and sound effects inside the game.
  • You can manage tracking permission in iOS Settings.
  • You can delete the app to remove locally stored game progress and settings from your device.
  • You can limit ad tracking and reset advertising identifiers through iOS privacy settings where available.

9. Children's Privacy

LumaRing Hop is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

10. Data Retention

Local gameplay data remains on your device until you delete it through app behavior, reset device data where available, or uninstall the app. Third-party providers may retain information according to their own retention policies.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the effective date above. Continued use of the game after an update means you accept the revised policy.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the support contact listed on the App Store product page for LumaRing Hop.
